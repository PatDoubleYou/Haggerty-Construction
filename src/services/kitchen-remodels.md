Your kitchen is where your family actually lives — not the living room, not the dining room. It's where the homework gets done, where the coffee gets poured, and where everybody ends up standing around during the party.

So when it stops working for you — when the layout is fighting you, the cabinets are falling apart, or the counters make you cringe every morning — it's time.

## We handle kitchen remodels start to finish

We're a full-service general contractor. That means you don't need to hire a separate demo crew, plumber, electrician, tile guy, and painter. We coordinate the entire project under one roof, with one point of contact, and one timeline you can actually trust.

We've remodeled kitchens across St. George, Washington, and the greater Washington County area — from simple cabinet-and-countertop refreshes to full gut renovations with new layouts, islands, and custom millwork.

## Why clients choose us for their kitchen

**We're honest about what things cost.** You'll get a real estimate with real numbers before we start. We use our own proprietary software, Haggerty OS, to keep the project organized and the budget transparent.

**We care about the details.** Tight seams on the countertops. Level cabinets. Clean grout lines. Properly aligned hardware. That's the stuff that separates a good kitchen from a great one — and we don't cut corners on it.

**We communicate.** You'll always know what's happening, what's next, and whether we're on schedule. If something changes, you hear about it from us — not from a subcontractor you've never met.
