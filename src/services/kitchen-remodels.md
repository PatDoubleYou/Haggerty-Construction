A kitchen remodel usually means new cabinets and appliances, and often a lot more: lighting changes, moved outlets, backsplash and countertops, paint. Sometimes flooring. Sometimes reframing for a new layout, or running a vent hood out through the roof.

It can get complicated fast. Every one of those pieces depends on another one, and the order matters.

That's why we plan it slow. Before demo, we settle your selections, check every appliance spec against the cabinet openings and the power, and put the lead times on the schedule. Then the build runs smooth.
