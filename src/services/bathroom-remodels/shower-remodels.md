Your shower is the one thing in your bathroom you use every single day. If it is outdated tile from the '90s, a fiberglass insert that is yellowing, or a tub-shower combo that nobody actually uses as a tub — it is time.

A shower remodel does not have to mean ripping out your entire bathroom. Sometimes the biggest impact comes from replacing just the shower — new tile, a glass enclosure, better fixtures, and proper waterproofing that will last decades instead of years.

## What makes a great shower remodel

**It starts with waterproofing.** This is the part most homeowners never think about — and the part where cutting corners causes the most expensive problems. Every shower we build gets a full waterproofing membrane behind the tile. No exceptions.

**The tile work has to be precise.** Shower tile is unforgiving. A line that is off by an eighth of an inch is visible every morning. Grout joints that are inconsistent look sloppy forever. We take the time to get it right — every cut, every corner, every transition.

**The details make it feel finished.** A properly sloped niche that drains instead of pooling water. A linear drain that sits perfectly flush. Glass that is measured to the millimeter. These are the things that separate a contractor-grade shower from one that actually feels custom.
