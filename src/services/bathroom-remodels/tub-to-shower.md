Most tub-shower combos go unused as tubs. The kids outgrew it. You take showers. And every morning you step over a high wall into a cramped space with a shower head from 2003.

A tub-to-shower conversion is one of the most impactful bathroom upgrades you can do — more usable space, better accessibility, modern fixtures, and a shower that looks like it belongs in your home.

## This is not a one-day bath company job

We remove the tub entirely. The plumbing gets modified, the subfloor gets inspected, and the space gets rebuilt from scratch with real waterproofing, real tile, and real craftsmanship. When we are done, it does not look like a converted tub — it looks like the shower was always supposed to be there.

## What the conversion involves

**Plumbing changes are almost always needed.** Valve position, drain location, and supply lines typically all change. We coordinate it all and make sure every change is done to code.

**Waterproofing is not optional.** Every conversion gets a full membrane system to prevent moisture damage. We never skip this step.

**The tile is where it gets personal.** Subway, large format, natural stone, mosaic accents — we help you choose and install with precision.

## Walk-in and curbless options

For accessibility — now or in the future — we offer curbless designs with linear drains that provide barrier-free entry. They look great and serve a real functional purpose.
