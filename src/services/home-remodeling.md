Sometimes a house just doesn't fit anymore. The kitchen is too small, the bathrooms are dated, the layout wastes space, or the finishes are stuck in a decade you'd rather forget. That's when it's time to remodel — not just patch things up, but actually make the home work for the way you live now.

We're a full-service general contractor in St. George, Utah, and home remodeling is what we do. One point of contact, and a hand-picked network of trusted local craftsmen — every trade held to our standards and coordinated by our team from demo to final walkthrough. Demo, framing, plumbing, electrical, cabinetry, tile, paint, and trim, all managed under one honest estimate.

## What makes us different

**We built our own project management software.** It's called Haggerty OS, and it means your project is tracked with a level of precision that most contractors can't match. You get a detailed estimate with real line items, a clear timeline, and updates that actually keep you informed.

**We're a family business.** William, Blake, and Jayden Haggerty — that's who you're working with. You work directly with us, not a rotating cast of strangers. We hand-pick every craftsman on your project and hold them to our standards, because our reputation in St. George matters to us personally and we protect it on every job.

**We serve all of Washington County.** St. George, Washington, Hurricane, Ivins, Santa Clara, LaVerkin, Leeds — if you're in the area, we'd love to talk about your project.

## Every project gets the same care

Whether you're updating a single room or renovating an entire home, we bring the same standards. Real communication. Clean job sites. Detailed estimates. And a final walkthrough where nothing gets signed off until you're happy.

That's not a sales pitch — it's just how we work.
