A whole-home remodel is usually a kitchen, a bathroom or two, and the rooms in between: flooring, paint, trim, maybe a built-in. None of it is exotic. What makes it hard is that it all happens at once, in a house you're still living in.

The cabinets can't go in until the floor is down. The floor can't go down until the subfloor is fixed. The counters can't be templated until the cabinets are set, and the painter needs to be out before any of it. One late delivery and three rooms wait.

That's the job. Not any one trade, but the order of all of them. So we plan it slow: every selection for every room, every lead time, the sequence, and the surprises we're likely to find. Then one person, Blake, runs the whole thing on one schedule, and you have one number to call.
