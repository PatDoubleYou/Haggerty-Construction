Off-the-shelf cabinets come in sizes that almost fit. Custom ones fit. That sounds like a small difference until you see a wall of built-ins with no filler strips, no gap at the ceiling, and every shelf at the height of the thing that goes on it.

Built-ins are where we started, and we still do a lot of them: bookcases, home offices, mudrooms, living room walls, garages, and the cabinet packages inside our kitchen remodels. We design the piece to your wall, a local manufacturer we trust mills the boxes and doors to our specs, and we deliver and install it ourselves. One crew answers for the whole thing.

We've also modified a lot of other people's cabinets to make a new fridge or range fit. That's most of why we read the appliance specs before anything gets built.
