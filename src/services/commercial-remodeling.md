Your business space says something before you even open your mouth. Whether it's an office that's outgrown its layout, a retail storefront that needs a fresh look, or a leased space that needs to be built out from scratch — how it's designed and constructed matters.

We bring the same detail-obsessed approach to commercial projects that we bring to our residential work. The difference is scale and scheduling — but the craftsmanship stays the same.

## Why businesses in St. George choose us

**We work around your schedule.** We know that construction during business hours can cost you revenue. We plan our work to minimize disruption — including evenings and weekends when needed.

**We handle the paperwork.** Commercial permitting, code compliance, ADA requirements, fire inspections — we manage the logistics so you can focus on running your business.

**We're honest about scope.** We focus on small to mid-size commercial renovations. If your project is a 50,000 square-foot build, we'll tell you that upfront and point you in the right direction. If it's a 2,000 square-foot office renovation, tenant improvement, or retail build-out — that's our sweet spot.

## Our commercial experience

Our founder, William Haggerty, spent years at Baker Concrete working on major commercial projects — including the Cincinnati Reds Stadium. That background in large-scale construction gave us the systems-level thinking we bring to every project, even smaller-scale commercial work.

Today, we apply those principles with our proprietary project management software, Haggerty OS, to keep commercial projects on schedule and on budget. You'll get clear communication, documented progress, and no surprises at the invoice.
