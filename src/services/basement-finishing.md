You have a basement. Right now it might be raw concrete, exposed framing, maybe some storage boxes and a water heater. But it is also hundreds of square feet of livable space — the kind of space that most homeowners would pay a fortune to add on to their house. Except you already have it. It just needs to be finished.

We are a full-service general contractor in St. George, and basement finishing is one of the most practical, high-value projects a homeowner can take on. You get new living space without changing your home's footprint, without a foundation pour, and without months of exterior construction.

## What we bring to basement projects

**We handle everything in-house.** Framing, plumbing, electrical, drywall, flooring, tile, paint, and trim — all under one crew with one point of contact. You do not need to coordinate separate trades yourself.

**We know the code.** Basement bedrooms in Utah require egress windows of a specific size. Bathrooms need proper drainage and ventilation. Electrical requires dedicated circuits. We handle all of this — including permits and inspections — so you do not have to worry about it.

**We are honest about what makes sense.** Not every basement needs to become a five-room suite. Sometimes the best use of the space is one really well-done room. We will help you figure out the right scope for your goals and your budget.

## A note about foundations

We finish and remodel basements. We do not do foundation repair, structural work, or major waterproofing. If your basement has foundation issues, we will tell you — and we will recommend a specialist who can help. Once the structure is solid, that is where we come in.
