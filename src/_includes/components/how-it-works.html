How it works

We plan it before we build it.

Four steps, and you always know which one you're on.

  1. Free detailed estimate

    We walk your space and send a real, line-item estimate. We built our own software to put estimates together, so you usually have it within a few days.

    Free

  2. Pre-construction

    We sit down together and finish the plan: every selection, the lead times, the risks, a realistic schedule, and a budget dialed in close.

    ~3%, credited to your build

  3. Your Project Success Plan

    Scope, budget, selections and schedule, all in one document. It's yours to keep. No obligation to build with us.

  4. We build it

    You sign the contract, we launch, and the plan does what plans are supposed to do.

Want to see what you'd get?

We'll send you a real example, so you can see exactly what the plan looks like before you commit to anything.
