Our Story

The Haggerty name has been tied to construction for generations. Our grandfather, Patrick R. Haggerty, managed construction logistics for the U.S. Antarctic Program — the kind of projects where failure isn't an option and the weather is trying to kill you. They named Haggerty Hill after him. That's the standard we come from.

Our founder, William, served over 20 years in the National Guard and spent years at Baker Concrete working on massive projects — including the Cincinnati Reds' Great American Ball Park. When he brought his family to St. George, he brought all of that experience with him and turned it into something personal: a small construction company built on doing things right, not doing things fast.

Today, Haggerty Construction is run by William and his sons. Blake leads project management on the ground. Jayden builds the custom technology that keeps every job organized and transparent. Together, we take on residential remodels that matter to the people who live in them — kitchens, bathrooms, basements, cabinetry, and everything in between.

We're not trying to be the biggest contractor in Southern Utah. We're trying to be the one you trust.

Pioneer Roots. Local Commitment.

Our family didn't just move to St. George — we helped build it. The Haggertys have pioneer heritage in this valley, and our roots in Washington County go back generations. This is our community, our neighborhood, our home.

That matters when you're choosing a contractor. We're not a franchise that rolled in from Las Vegas. We're not a crew you'll never see again after the job is done. We live here. Our kids go to school here. Our reputation in this town is the only marketing we need.

When we say we'll take care of you, we mean it — because we'll see you at the grocery store next week.

Disabled Veteran-Owned

William Haggerty served over two decades in the National Guard. That service shaped everything about how we run this company — discipline, accountability, attention to detail, and a deep sense of responsibility to the people who depend on us.

Being a disabled veteran-owned business isn't a marketing badge for us. It's part of who we are. The same values that drove William's service drive the way we approach every project: show up, do the work right, and leave it better than you found it.
