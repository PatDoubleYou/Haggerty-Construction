The old kitchen didn't have room for the appliances the owners wanted or the storage they needed, so this one came down to the studs and got a new layout.

The room is built around a big knotty alder island with a white quartz top, with a wall of full-height alder pantry cabinets where the old uppers used to be. Alder is a warm, knotty wood that hides daily use well, and it's a good match for a kitchen that actually gets cooked in.

What we planned up front: every appliance spec checked against the cabinet openings and the power before anything was built, and the cabinet lead time on the schedule, so the kitchen wasn't torn out for weeks waiting on boxes.
