# Questions for Blake

Blake runs the jobs, so these are his to answer. Answer however is easiest:
voice memo, text, or type into this file. Rough is fine. Don't polish it;
the way you'd say it to a client in their kitchen is exactly what we want.

## Kitchens
1. When you walk a kitchen for an estimate, what are you looking for that
   most contractors miss?
2. Tell me about a kitchen where planning up front saved the client a
   headache. What would have gone wrong?
3. What's a question clients should ask every contractor, that most don't?

## Bathrooms
4. What's the worst thing you've found behind a shower wall, and how did the
   client take it (given you'd warned them it might be there)?
5. What do you tell someone who's comparing us to a one-day tub-liner company?

## Whole-home
6. On the Kraai job, what was the hardest part to coordinate, and how did you
   keep it on schedule?
7. How do you keep a family sane when they're living in the house during a
   remodel?

## Working with you

9. What's your favorite part of the job?

## Photos
