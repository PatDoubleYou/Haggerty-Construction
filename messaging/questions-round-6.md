# Round 6: whole-home, cabinetry, projects, gallery

Voice memo these the way you'd say them to a client at their kitchen table.
Rough is fine. The whole-home, custom cabinetry, gallery and three project
pages were drafted from the workbook and the photos, not from your words,
so they read like a good impression of you. These answers replace that.

If you only answer five: 1, 4, 6, 8 and 14.

## Whole-home (the money page)
1. Walk me through the Kraai job from the first walkthrough to handing over
   the keys. What order did the rooms go in, and why that order?
2. What was the one thing that nearly went sideways, and what did you do?
3. How did they live in the house while you were in it? What did you set up
   so they could?
4. If a couple with a 1990s kitchen and two tired baths called tomorrow,
   what would you tell them a job like that costs and how long it takes?
   Say the numbers you'd actually say.
5. Can we name the Kraais and use the photos? If yes, what would they say
   about you if I asked them?

## Custom cabinetry
6. Tell me the green bookcase story the way you'd tell it at a party. Where
   was it, who was it for, what went wrong.
7. Walk me through what happens between "we want a built-in" and the day
   it's installed. Who measures, who draws it, who mills it, who finishes
   it, who installs it.
8. Which pieces do you actually build yourselves right now, and which go to
   the manufacturer?
9. The mudroom, the blue office, the light bookcase with the dinosaurs, the
   garage bench: pick two and tell me what the client wanted and what you
   talked them into or out of.
10. What's the question people ask about custom cabinets that you wish
    they'd stop asking, and what's the one they should ask?

## The three projects
11. The knotty alder kitchen: whose house, what was wrong with the old
    kitchen, what did you plan for up front, what did it cost roughly, how
    long did it take.
12. The mudroom: same questions. Why rift-cut oak, and was that your idea
    or theirs?
13. Anything you'd rather not have on a project page, so it never gets
    written.

## Gallery and photos
14. Which five photos on the site make you proud, and which five make you
    wince? The first group leads, the second gets cut.
15. Is there a finished-room photo from the Kraai house that isn't on the
    site yet?
