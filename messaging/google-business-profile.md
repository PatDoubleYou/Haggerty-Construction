## 2. Services

Google weighs the predefined services (lowercase, suggested by Google) more
than custom ones, so use the predefined one wherever it exists and add a
custom one only for things Google has no name for. Every custom service
takes a description up to 300 characters; Google reads it.

Under General contractor: Basement remodeling, Drywall installation, Drywall
repair, Drywall Repair, Home repairs, Painting, Tile work installation,

Our first real job, April 2021. A floor-to-ceiling bookcase, painted green, sprayed in Dad's driveway because that's where we had room. It's still standing. Everything we've built since is built the same way: to the wall, to the ceiling, to what you actually own.

Kitchen, laundry room and two bathrooms in one house, finished on schedule. Cabinets to the ceiling, the fridge built into a wall of pantry storage, a coffee bar and a farmhouse sink. The only extensions were changes the owners asked for, and every one was written down.

## 4. Monthly routine (20 minutes)

- Reply to every new review within a week. Two sentences, mention the job.
- Upload four photos: two finished rooms, one in progress, one of the crew.
- Post twice. Reuse a site photo; three sentences; the estimate button.
- Text the review link at every closeout. That is the whole review strategy.
- Check the Performance tab once. If "searches breakdown" grows past 22
  terms and starts showing cabinet queries, the category change worked.
